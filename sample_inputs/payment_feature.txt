Feature: Payment Processing

As a customer, I want to pay for my order using a credit/debit card
so that I can complete my purchase on the e-commerce platform.

Acceptance Criteria:
- User must be logged in to access the payment page
- Accepted card types: Visa, MasterCard, RuPay
- Card number must be 16 digits
- CVV must be 3 digits
- Expiry date must be in the future
- On successful payment: show order confirmation with order ID
- On payment failure: show "Payment failed. Please try again."
- UPI payment option should also be available
- Transaction must complete within 30 seconds
- Email receipt should be sent after successful payment
